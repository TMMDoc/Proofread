Multi-touch

TruTops Boost supports Microsoft® touch technology for interaction with designs using a stylus or contact fingers on a touchscreen.

Multi-touch is not available in all areas and supplements the mouse rather than replacing it.

Multi-touch is available for:

  • Boost tools: Turn, Move, Zoom, Rotate

  • Radial menu

  • Touch gestures

With multiple screens, touch input is directed to the screen defined as the touchscreen in the operating system.

Extend application window

The application window can extend across two multi-touch monitors. To avoid multi-touch problems (for example, a contact finger drag creating a box only on the other screen), configure each display individually.

  1. Set up the multi-touch screen on the primary display.

  2. Configure the second display.

  3. Save the setting.

The TruTops Boost window can be moved between displays using multi-touch.

Multi-touch with the "Drag" tool

  1. To select a shape and change it, touch the screen and move the contact finger or stylus across the screen.

  2. To highlight the shape, drag immediately, or touch and drag an empty area. The selection is previewed before it is applied.

  3. To select the shape, tap with a second contact finger or lift the first contact finger.

  4. To enter dimensions for an offset surface, press the space bar.

  5. To run through available clearance contours (equivalent to tapping twice), pull a contact finger out of an empty area, or move a contact finger over an edge and tap twice with another contact finger.

  6. To select all surfaces of the body (equivalent to tapping three times), pull a contact finger out of an empty area, or move a contact finger over a surface and tap three times with another contact finger.

Multi-touch with the "Move" tool

  1. Select the faces, bodies, or components to move or rotate.

  2. Select another object to define the direction.

  3. Attach the Move tool by pulling the yellow ball into the center.

  4. Tap to enable a rotation or linear axis.

  5. Pull to move or rotate the shape.

Perform the tap or pull with a stylus, or with a contact finger by dragging and lifting.
