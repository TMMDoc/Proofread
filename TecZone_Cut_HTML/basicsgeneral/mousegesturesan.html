Mouse gestures and touch gestures

Mouse gestures and touch gestures

Mouse and touch gestures are shortcuts for frequent actions and tools. Gesture information is shown in the design window and on the status bar during execution.

Mouse gestures are fully supported in TecZone Design. Support is limited in TecZone Bend and TecZone Cut.

Overview of touch gestures

Touch gestures: Navigation
Action Description

Move

Move two contact fingers over the screen.

Zoom

  • To zoom in, move two contact fingers away from each other.

  • To zoom out, move two contact fingers towards each other.

Rotate

Hold a contact finger on the axis to rotate it, then move the other contact finger in a circle around it.

Rotate

Move three contact fingers over the screen.

Rotation around an axis

Hold one contact finger on the axis to rotate it and move two contact fingers on the screen.

Determining the view

Use three contact fingers to activate the "Determine view" tool.

  • While the "Snap View" tool is enabled, tapping acts as a left mouse click and stroking as a drag with the left mouse button.

  • To deactivate the tool, hold three contact fingers again or press Esc.

Performing a gesture

  1. To perform a mouse gesture, hold down the right mouse button and drag the gesture.

  2. To perform a touch gesture, touch the touchscreen with two contact fingers or a stylus and drag the gesture.

  3. To cancel a gesture, do not move the mouse, contact finger or stylus for one second.
