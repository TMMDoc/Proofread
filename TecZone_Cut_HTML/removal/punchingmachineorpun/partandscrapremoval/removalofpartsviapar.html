A colored square indicates that parts are removed via a part removal flap or part chute. The maximum part size depends on the machine. The respective size of the part removal flap, part chute or laser die can be displayed in the configuration of the removal of small parts.

Programmable support table option for TruPunch 1000 (S05), TruPunch 2000 (S18) and TruPunch 3000 (S11): Up to 4 different part chutes are available. Each part chute is suitable for a different maximum part size and is marked in color.

  • - Removal via part chute with part size up to max. 180 x 150 mm.

  • - Removal via part chute with part size up to max. 460 x 150 mm (S05 and S18) or 500 x 200 mm (S11).

  • - Removal via part chute with part size up to max. 460 x 500 mm (S05 and S18) or 500 x 500 mm (S11).

During removal via punching flap or part chute, the part can be pushed out to optimally position it on the part removal flap or part chute. Parts can be pushed out when the part removal flap is open.

Only push out parts if the sheet thickness is s > 1.5 mm. Otherwise, there is a risk of the part sliding under the scrap skeleton. Parts with a sheet thickness of s < 1.5 mm can be pushed out by a spring-loaded stripper.

remains

Normal

Removing a scrap part

A scrap part is an area on the sheet that can be disposed of under the control of the machine. In the separating sequence the scrap part is treated like a finished part or cut out. Machines that have a scrap sorting can immediately treat the scrap part as scrap.

Use the variable AutoScrapAreaCreation in the machine rules to determine whether closed scrap surfaces are automatically defined as a scrap part for inner contours.

If the selected separation stroke is not on the scrap part which was determined, then processing operations can be marked, which are to be ignored when recalculating the geometry of the scrap part. This is helpful, if the separation stroke is overlapped by a nibbling path which is to be executed afterwards.

With TruMatic 3000 (K09) and TruPunch 3000 (S20), the parts conveyor belt with switching function is available (option). To do this, the option Switch (programmable in 2 positions) must be activated in the machine configuration.

  • The switching function controls:

  • Finished parts or scrap parts fall into the box below the part chute.

  • Good parts or scrap parts are transported via the conveyor belt.

Removing large scrap parts

If large scrap parts are to be removed via the part removal flap or part chute, the scrap must be cut into small pieces prior to removal.

The scrap is automatically cut into small pieces. The size of the scrap parts is calculated based on the size of the selected part removal flap or part chute. Then, processing operations are created between the scrap areas and between the scrap area and the finished parts with the active tool.

  • Processing operations that protrude from the sheet margin are correspondingly lengthened.

  • Arcs must be processed.

  • Long nibble processing operations are correspondingly split on the basis of created scrap parts.

  • Disposals at inner contours are deleted and re-created for the scrap areas.

  • Disposals at outer contours are retained.

  • If there are processing operations in the compulsory sequence, these are taken out of the existing compulsory sequence before the splitting.

  1. Activate a suitable punching tool.

  2. Press tab Removal > Flap, button Scrap area.

    • The drawing of the part removal flap or part chute is displayed.

  3. If several options for removal are available: select part removal flap or part chute in the folding drawing displayed.

  4. Select the inner contour or draw a selection frame around the scrap area.

    • The scrap area is split up according to the size of the selected part removal flap or part chute.

Removing parts in the case of open part removal flap

The following symbols show the opening and closing of the part removal flap:

  • - The part removal flap remains open after removal.

  • - Remove parts in the case of open part removal flap.

  • - Remove parts if the part removal flap is open and close the part removal flap after removal of parts.

  • Only disposals created with the Removal with open flap option are taken into account when the function is programmed.

  • The function is only programmed during automatic optimization.

  • With the NC cycle Switch flap…​, parts are also removed when the part removal flap is continuously open. The NC cycle is used to subsequently change a sheet already optimized, however.
Requirement

  • The removal has an outer contour reference.

  • If the removal was created manually, the Removal with open flap option must have been activated in the parameters for removing small parts.

  1. Press tab Optimization > Optimize, button Start.

    • The dialog Processing optimization opens.

  2. Select Tool change opti. or Process row by row.

  3. Select Separate row by row.

  4. Select optimize for open flap.

  5. Press the Continue button.

  6. Change the tool sequence if necessary.

  7. Press the OK button.

    • The symbols for opening or closing the part removal flap are displayed on the removal symbols.

Change SortMaster Box Linear container configuration

Three standard configurations with two large-sized, four medium-sized and six small-sized small parts containers are available for selection.

  1. Part gate (option)

  2. SortMaster Box Linear

Requirement:

  • SortMaster Box Linear is assigned as small part sorting to the machine.

  • The container configuration has been imported.

  1. Press tab Removal > Flap, button Set.

  2. Press tool guide SETTINGS Change SortMaster Box Linear parameters.

    • The dialog Selection container configuration opens.

  3. In the field Container configuration, press .

  4. Select the desired configuration.

  5. Press the OK button.

After a change of the container configuration, all affected flap removals can be deleted. Other removal methods are retained.
