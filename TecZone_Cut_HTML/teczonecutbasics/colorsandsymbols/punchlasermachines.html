Punch machine/Punch laser machine

The following colors and symbols information is for punching machine or punch laser machine.

Colors and lines

Blue

  • Punch processing

  • Solid:

    • Positioning paths between machined parts

    • Only for machines with additional axes on the laser cutting head: Laser processing by moving the X/Y axes only. With these machines, laser processing is created by moving the X/Y axes and the additional axes.

  • Dashed: For punch laser machine: Overshoot range of the machine’s punching station

  • Triangle (arrow): In connection with positioning motion:

    • Direction of the positioning motion

    • Starting point for the next processing

  • Cross: Tool center for punch processing

Cyan

  • Solid: Positioning path

  • For punch laser machines: Solid: Processing type vaporizing without cutting

Yellow

  • Circle: Collision during processing

  • Cross: Tool center when processing with presser foot

  • Solid: Corner treatment

Green

  • Solid:

    • Laser processing

    • Machines with additional axes on the laser cutting head: Laser processing with superimposed movements of the X/Y axes and additional axes of the laser cutting head

  • Dashed: Processings in a compulsory sequence

  • Cross: Tool center when processing with delayed single stroke

Green and red

Triangle (arrow): Machines with additional axes on the laser cutting head - FinalCut

Purple

Optimize first punching operation in the positioning sequence with interactive optimization.

Magenta

  • Solid:

    • Selected laser processing

    • Unprocessed geometries or contours

  • Dashed:

    • With punching machines: Processing station overshoot range of the machine

    • With punch laser machines: Laser station overshoot range of the machine

  • Selected punch processing

Red

  • Solid:

    • Approach flag for laser processing

    • Machines with additional axes on the laser cutting head: Laser processing by moving the additional axes

    • Machines with additional axes on the laser cutting head: Laser processing with FinalCut

    • Path of the GripMaster roller during the removal of scrap skeletons

    • For punch laser machines: positioning lines if no bypass path can be determined.

  • Arrow: Repositioning

  • Square: shaded; Line: solid: Dead area on the sheet caused by a light barrier (default setting), a lift pedestal, etc.

  • Circle or rectangle: Index pin with or without sensor

  • Square: Optical index pin

  • Arrow: Repositioning of active clamping

Brown

Continuous: Contour elements that have been defined as visible edges in the design of a part.
