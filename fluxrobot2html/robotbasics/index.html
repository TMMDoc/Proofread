Robot Basics

Accurate robot motion depends on knowing three things: where the robot is, what tool it is holding, and where each station in the cell is located.

This section explains the above three things in detail, including the coordinate systems used to define the robot’s position, the tool’s position, and the positions of the cell’s stations. It also explains how to calibrate the robot’s tool and the cell’s stations so that the robot can accurately move to the correct positions during operation.
