Safety Precautions

Safety Precautions

Read and understand all safety precautions in this document before operating, programming, or performing maintenance on the robot cell. Failure to follow these precautions may result in serious injury or death.

This chapter defines the safety precautions applicable to the entire FluxRobot documentation set. These precautions cover all aspects of the system - from initial setup and calibration through day-to-day operation, offline programming, and maintenance. The FluxRobot cell consists of a FANUC robot arm, a press brake machine, and associated peripheral stations enclosed within a safety fence.

Personnel Safety

Authorized Personnel Only

  • Only trained and authorized personnel are permitted to operate, program, or maintain the robot cell.

  • Operators must complete robot-specific safety training before working with or near the cell.

  • Unauthorized personnel must not enter the fenced robot work area under any circumstances.

Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

Wear appropriate PPE at all times when working near the robot cell:

  • Safety shoes to protect against falling objects.

  • Protective gloves when handling sheet metal or tooling.

Working Near the Robot

  • Never stand in the path of a moving robot arm or within its reach envelope unless the robot is in a safe, stopped state.

  • Always be aware of the robot’s programmed motion path before entering the work area.

  • Never assume the robot is stopped - verify the robot status on the teach pendant before approaching.

Emergency Stop

Emergency Stop Locations

Emergency buttons are red and mushroom-shaped for easy identification. The FluxRobot Cell is equipped with multiple emergency stop buttons:

  • Teach Pendant E-Stop

  • Controller E-Stop

  • Press Brake E-Stop

  • Foot Pedal E-Stop

Activating Emergency Stop

  1. Press any emergency stop button immediately if a hazardous situation arises.

  2. The robot will enter Immediate Stop mode and all motion will cease.

  3. The stack light will indicate RED when the emergency stop is active.

Recovering from Emergency Stop

Before releasing an emergency stop, ensure the cause of the stop has been identified and resolved. Never release an emergency stop without understanding why it was activated.

  1. Identify and eliminate the cause of the emergency stop.

  2. Twist and release the activated emergency stop button to reset it.

  3. Acknowledge any fault messages on the teach pendant.

  4. Press the RESET button on the teach pendant to clear errors.

  5. Resume operation only after confirming the FluxRobot Cell is safe.

Safety Fence

General Rules

  • Keep all fence doors closed and latched during auto mode operation.

  • Inspect the fence and door switches for damage before each shift. Report any damage immediately and do not operate the FluxRobot Cell until repaired.

  • Do not reach through the fence mesh or openings while the robot is in auto mode.

Entering the Fenced Area

  1. Switch the robot to T1 mode before entering the fenced area.

  2. Inform other personnel that you are entering the fenced area.

  3. Carry the teach pendant with the deadman switch engaged.

  4. Perform only the required task, then exit and close the fence door.

Press Brake Safety

  • Never place hands or fingers inside the press brake bending area during operation.

  • The press brake and robot operate in a coordinated sequence - do not interrupt or manually override the press brake while the robot is holding a part.

  • Ensure the press brake emergency stops are functional before starting production.

  • Do not override or disable the press brake guards or safety devices.

General Do’s and Don’ts

Do’s

  • Always verify the robot status before entering the work area.

  • Keep the teach pendant accessible at all times during manual operation.

  • Report any unusual robot behaviour, sounds, or vibrations to a supervisor immediately.

  • Keep the cell floor around the robot dry and free of obstacles.

  • Ensure all personnel are aware when a program is about to start.

Don’ts

  • Do not disable or bypass any safety device, interlock, or guard.

  • Do not operate the robot while fatigued, distracted, or impaired.

  • Do not allow non-authorised personnel to operate or enter the robot cell.

  • Do not make modifications to the robot program without proper authorisation and documentation.

  • Do not ignore alarms or fault messages - investigate and resolve them before resuming operation.

For full safety specifications and regulatory compliance details, refer to the FANUC Robot Series R-30iB/R-30iB Plus Controller Operator’s Manual and the relevant press brake manufacturer’s safety documentation.
