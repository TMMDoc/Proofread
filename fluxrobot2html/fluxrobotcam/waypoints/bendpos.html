Bend Positioning

Bend Positioning

Phase Image Flux output Motion Coordinates

Pre-bend safe (Not applicable for bend 1)

The gripper moves away from the machine to this safe position to proceed the next bend

JJ

Station Front (Not applicable for bend1)

It is a ready position where the gripper holds the part in front of the bending machine.

LC

Part Insert

The gripper moves to the part insert position which is approximately 50 to 100 mm away from the bending machine’s tool.

JC

Part Insert 2

The gripper holds the part and positions it between the bending machine’s tool.

LC

Mute point

The gripper places the part on the die tool against the backgauge.

LC

Pinch point

The part’s bend line gets placed accurately on the middle of die tool’s V width and the punch tool contacts the part after backgauge alignment.

LC

Retract gauges

After reaching pinch point, the backgauge gets retracted.

LC

Bending

During this phase, Syncronized bending process occurs.

LC

Over Bending

The part is overbent to compensate springback.

LC

Retract

After overbending, The gripper releases the part and retracts.

LC

Swivel 1

Post retraction, the gripper swivels to the next position.

LC

Swivel 2

From Swivel 1, gripper moves towards the part to perform regripping.

LC

Contact

The gripper moves towards the part to make contact, the vaccum gets turned ON and the part gets suctioned.

LC

Decompress

RAM moves slightly upwards causing the sheet to deflect. To compensate the deflection, the gripper moves backwards.

LC

Opening

RAM goes fully up, gripper then lifts the part above the die tool.

LC

Post Bend Safe

Gripper holding the part moves little away from the machine.

LC

Post Bend Safe 2

Gripper moves away from the machine to this safe position to proceed transition from bending to next phase.

JJ
In the above table, the regripping process occurs during the Retract phase and Contact phase. These simulation phases occurs only when regripping is chosen. If the part can be bent without regripping, then the Retract phase to Contact phase gets skipped and the simulation occurs from Overbending phase to Decompress phase.
