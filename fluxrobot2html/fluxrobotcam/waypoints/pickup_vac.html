Pickup Pallet

Pickup Pallet

Phase Image Flux output Motion Coordinates

Pre Pickup Safe

Gripper moves from home position to a safe distance from the maximum stack height.

JC

Approach

Minimum of 50 mm above the calculated stack height.

LC

Contact

Gripper contacts the sheet which is at the top of the stack. Once the sheet is detected, the vaccum gets turned ON and the part gets suctioned.

LC

Separate

Gripper picks the sheet at the top of the stack then moves from stack to a certain mm to separate it.

LC

LiftClear

After separating phase, the gripper moves to a safe height in a linear motion.

LC

Pickup transition swing

From lift clear position, the gripper begins the transition from pickup pallet to centering table.

JC
